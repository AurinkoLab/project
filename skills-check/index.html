Skills Check

Before the build starts, have a go at reading a real drawing from the project. Try it at home, at your own pace, and bring what you worked out to our session.

This is not an exam and you cannot fail it. We are not sorting people into good and bad — we are finding out what each person already knows, so that nobody spends the first week bored and nobody spends it lost.

Before you start

  • A ruler with millimetres on it
  • Paper and a pencil — write your answers down, don't keep them in your head
  • About 20 minutes, somewhere quiet

Print the drawing if you can. Reading it on paper is easier than on a phone, and you can write on it.

How far should I get?

Part 1 is straightforward once you know where to look. Part 2 needs a bit of arithmetic. Part 3 is genuinely difficult and most people will not finish it — that is expected, and there is a self-check at the end of the questions so you can test your own numbers.

If you get stuck after two questions, come to the session anyway. Turning up having tried is worth far more to us than turning up with perfect answers.

What we are actually looking at

Honestly? Not how many you got right.

  • Whether you say "I don't know" instead of making something up
  • Whether safety crosses your mind before we mention it
  • Whether you noticed something odd about the drawing and said so
  • Whether you would tell someone if you made a mistake on a real part

Those four things matter more in a workshop than any amount of previous experience with tools. Experience we can teach in a fortnight.
