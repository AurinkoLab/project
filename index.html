Your Build Journey 🏁

Months 1–2

Basics & Frame Blueprinting

Understanding vehicle dynamics, structural integrity, chassis design, and safety protocols.

Months 3–4

Electric Drivetrain & Assembly

Motor installation, battery management systems (BMS), wiring harnesses, and braking mechanics.

Months 5–6

Testing & The Grand Final Races

Fine-tuning performance, telemetry setup, track safety checks, and official head-to-head racing.
